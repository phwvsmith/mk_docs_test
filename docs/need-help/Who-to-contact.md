# Who to Contact

If you’re unable to resolve an issue or need additional support, use the options below.

---

## ✅ Before Reaching Out

Before contacting someone, please check:

- **Formatting Guide** – for styling issues  
- **Common Issues** – for publishing or update problems  
- **FAQ** – for quick answers  

Many issues can be resolved quickly using these pages.

---

## 💬 Primary Support Channel

For general help or questions:

- Use your team’s shared communication channel (e.g. Teams / Slack)
- Include:
  - Link to the page you were editing
  - A short description of the issue

This helps others assist you faster.

---

## 👥 Content Maintainers

If your issue relates to specific content:

- Contact the team or person responsible for that area  
- This could be:
  - Data owners  
  - Project leads  
  - Subject matter experts  

---

## ⚙️ Technical Issues

For issues such as:

- Changes not publishing  
- Errors during editing  
- Broken site behaviour  

Contact the platform or engineering team responsible for maintaining the site.

---

## 🚨 When to Escalate

Reach out if:

- Your changes are not appearing after a few minutes  
- You see persistent errors  
- You are unsure how to resolve a conflict  
- Something appears broken or incorrect  

---

## ✅ What to Include When Asking for Help

To get faster support, include:

- Page link  
- What you were trying to do  
- What went wrong  
- (Optional) Screenshot  

---

## 💡 Tip

Most issues are quick to resolve.  
Providing clear details will help others assist you efficiently.
